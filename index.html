Privacy Policy

Nebula Clean · last updated August 3, 2026

1. Summary

Nebula Clean (“the app”) is a storage and device clean-up utility published by Cool App Helpdesk. It frees storage, clears cache, detects duplicates and similar photos, and manages background apps.

  • The app does not request the internet permission and is technically incapable of transmitting data off your device.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • All scanning happens on-device; nothing you scan is uploaded anywhere.

2. Data we collect

The app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no server, and no network access. It does not collect names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, or usage statistics.

3. Data stored on your device

To do its job the app reads information from your device. Everything stays on your device.

5. Network activity and third parties

The app does not request the INTERNET permission. It is technically incapable of transmitting data. It includes no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDKs. All third-party libraries used operate entirely on-device.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

No data is stored off your device. Scan results and settings remain on your device until you delete them inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Nebula Clean → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
